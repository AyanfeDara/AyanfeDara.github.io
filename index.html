01—SQL · E-commerce · 2026

Late orders get 1‑star reviews

70%of orders over a week late got a 1-star review

Using SQL on 99,441 orders from a Brazilian online store, I compared each delivery date with the customer’s review. Late orders were only 6.7% of deliveries but accounted for 37% of all 1-star reviews. The five worst states, all in the North-east, ran 14 to 21% late, against 4.5% in São Paulo.

What I’d doFix carriers and delivery estimates in those five states first, and flag orders that are at risk of running late before the customer notices.

02—Power BI · Energy · 2026

Africa doubled its electricity supply, but not per person

+20%electricity per person, while total generation grew 119%

I built a three-page Power BI report on 54 African countries from 2000 to 2024. Population grew 83%, so each person’s share only rose from 534 to 639 kWh a year. Of seven large economies, Nigeria has the least electricity per person, and its share from renewables fell from 38% to 25%.

What it meansTotal output is the wrong yardstick. Measured per person, Nigeria comes last of the seven, and Ethiopia has overtaken it.

03—Python · Telecom · 2026

Call one in five customers, reach half of those about to leave

50%of leaving customers reached by calling the riskiest 20%

I studied 7,043 telecom customers in Python. Those on month-to-month plans leave 15 times as often as those on two-year contracts (42.7% against 2.8%). I compared two models and used the simpler one, a logistic regression (score 0.85), to rank who to call first. The riskiest 20% hold half of the customers who leave and 55% of the revenue at risk.

What I’d doScore customers every month, offer the riskiest 20% a longer contract, and test the offer on a small group before rolling it out.

04—R · Retail · 2026

Forecasting US retail sales within 3%

3.2%average error on 24 months the model had not seen

I forecast monthly sales for five US store types in R, using Census data from 2010 to 2026. Online sales more than doubled since 2019, while clothing fell once inflation is taken into account. I tested three methods on two years they had not seen: exponential smoothing was off by 3.2% on average, against 5.7% for simply repeating last year.

What it meansOnline is forecast to grow 6.7% over the next 12 months and grocery to stay almost flat. Clothing is the hardest to forecast, so it needs the largest stock buffer.

05—R · Aviation · 2026

Evening flights leave late three times as often

3×as likely to leave late after 6pm as before 9am

I joined 435,352 flights from New York’s three airports in 2023 to hourly weather at each airport, in R. Rain, poor visibility and strong wind each raised the late rate by 6 to 9 points, but only one flight in eight left in rain. A logistic regression showed time of day matters most: holding airline, airport and weather constant, 10% of flights would leave late if all left before 9am, against 32% after 6pm.

What I’d doBuild more slack into the evening timetable, where delays carried forward from earlier flights pile up, and plan for bad-weather days separately.
