Question

Is Africa's electricity supply keeping up with its population, and where does Nigeria stand?

Approach

A three-page Power BI report on 54 countries, cleaned in Power Query and modelled as a star schema with DAX measures.

Finding

Population grew 83%, absorbing most new supply. Nigeria's renewable share fell from 38% to 25% as fossil generation tripled, while Kenya's rose from 40% to 92%.

Recommendation

Measure progress per person, not in total generation. Kenya shows supply can grow and get cleaner at once. [Draft, confirm with Peter]

Share of orders with a 1-star review

By delivery date against the estimate, 95,824 delivered orders

Hover or tap a bar for details.

Source: Olist Brazilian e-commerce public dataset, 2016 to 2018

Question

Revenue grew 141% year on year. What is hurting customer ratings?

Approach

Loaded 9 linked tables (99,441 orders) into PostgreSQL, checked data quality, then compared review scores with delivery delay and state.

Finding

Late orders were 6.7% of deliveries but produced 37% of 1-star reviews. The five worst states are all in the North-east, 14 to 21% late against 4.5% in São Paulo.

Recommendation

Fix carriers and delivery estimates in Alagoas, Maranhão, Sergipe, Piauí and Ceará first, and flag orders at risk of running late before the customer notices.

Source: IBM Telco customer churn dataset, 7,043 customers

Question

With a limited retention budget, which customers should get the offer?

Approach

Chi-square tests on 7,043 customers, then a logistic regression (ROC AUC 0.85 on unseen data) compared against a random forest.

Finding

Month-to-month customers leave 15 times as often as two-year customers (42.7% against 2.8%). The riskiest 20% hold half of all leavers and 55% of the revenue at stake.

Recommendation

Score customers every month, offer the riskiest 20% a move to a longer contract, and A/B test the offer before rolling it out.
