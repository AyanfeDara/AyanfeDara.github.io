01 · SQL

Late deliveries drive 1-star reviews

E-commerce sales and delivery analysis, 99,441 orders

Orders delivered more than a week late received a 1-star review 70% of the time. Late orders were only 6.7% of deliveries but produced 37% of all 1-star reviews, and the five worst states for late delivery are all in Brazil's North-east.

02 · Power BI

Africa doubled its electricity supply, but not per person

Power BI dashboard, 54 African countries, 2000 to 2024

Generation grew 119%, but population grew 83%, so electricity per person rose only 20%. Nigeria has the least electricity per person of seven peer economies, and its renewable share fell from 38% to 25% as fossil generation tripled.

  • Power Query
  • DAX

03 · Python

Find half of all leavers by contacting one customer in five

Customer churn analysis and model, 7,043 telecom customers

A quarter of customers left, taking 30.5% of monthly revenue. A logistic regression model (ROC AUC 0.85) ranks customers by risk, so a retention offer sent to the riskiest 20% reaches half of all leavers and 55% of the revenue at stake.

  • Python
  • pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • Chi-square tests
