02—Power BI · Energy · 2026

Africa doubled its electricity, but not per person

+20%power per person, while generation grew 119%

A three-page Power BI report on 54 countries, 2000–2024, modelled as a star schema. Population grew 83%, so supply per person only went from 534 to 639 kWh. Nigeria came lowest among its peers, and its renewable share fell from 38% to 25%.
